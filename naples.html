Three years of living here, condensed into a list. It is not a guide and it is not complete — it is the places I actually go back to, and it changes as they do.

pizza · pasta · panino · coffee · gelato

  • Umberto 1916 A little more refined. My pick: paccheri with baby octopus, black olives, capers, and Vesuvian tomatoes. Via Alabardieri, 30/31, 80121 Napoli · website · instagram
  • Cuccuma Caffè Huge plates of spaghetti made for sharing. My pick: sausage and tomato. Vico II Gravina, 1, 80134 Napoli · no website · instagram
